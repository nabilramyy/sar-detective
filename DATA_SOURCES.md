# Data sources and attribution

- Radar: NASA-ISRO NISAR Level-2 GCOV, accessed through NASA's Alaska Satellite Facility (ASF DAAC). Scene IDs and links are in data/scene_manifest.json.
- River line and street map: (c) OpenStreetMap contributors, Open Database License (ODbL), https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
- Flood reports used as supporting evidence: NPR (Oct 23, 2026) and AP via OPB (Nov 2, 2026). Links are in the website and report.
- Web map library: Leaflet (BSD-2-Clause).

## USGS river gauge
USGS NWIS site 09342500, San Juan River at Pagosa Springs, CO. Provisional instantaneous values, Oct 8 to Nov 1, 2026 (data/usgs_gauge.png, data/usgs_gauge.json). Public domain (U.S. Government).
