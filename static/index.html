What could explain the changes?

  • Blue along the river: wet banks, mud, flood debris, or a river channel that moved.
  • Red spots: ground that became smoother or drier, or water that settled.
  • Scattered dots far from the river: probably normal changes like snow, wet soil, forests or steep slopes, not the flood.

What supports this?

  • News reports say the San Juan River flooded Pagosa Springs and crested around Oct 23, 2026.
  • The changes line up with the river, and are much rarer away from it.

What we are not sure about

This map shows where the radar signal changed. It does not prove that a place flooded. Always follow official flood maps and local emergency guidance.
  • The "after" image was taken about 3 days after the flood peak, so some water may already have gone.
  • The two images were processed with different software versions.
  • No official flood map was available to check against yet.
